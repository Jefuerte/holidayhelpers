About Our Organization

Holiday Helpers is a community organization that helps provide Christmas gifts to children from low-income families. We work with volunteers, donors, and local partners to bring holiday joy to families who need support the most.

Our Mission

We aim to create a brighter, happier holiday season for children in need. Every year, we collect and distribute gifts so every child can experience the joy of Christmas.
